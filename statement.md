Cab Share Utility

PROBLEM STATEMENT:
Managing cab sharing among students and commuters can be difficult without a proper system. People often struggle to find cabmates, coordinate departure times, and share driver details. This project aims to solve that problem by providing a simple Python-based utility to store and retrieve cab information efficiently.



 SCOPE OF THE PROJECT:
- Provide a lightweight, console-based solution for cab sharing.  
- Store cabmate details such as name, driver’s phone number, destination, and departure time in arrays.  
- Allow users to add new cab entries dynamically.  
- Enable searching for cabs by destination.  
- Serve as a foundation for future enhancements like persistent storage or graphical interfaces.  



TARGET USERS:
University students, who frequently share cabs to campus.  
Office commuters, looking to coordinate rides.  
General users, who want a simple tool to organize cab sharing without complex apps.  



HIGH LEVEL FEATURES:
- Store cabmate details in separate arrays (name, phone number, destination, time).  
- Add new cab entries with user input.  
- Search functionality to find cabs by destination.  
- Simple text-based interface for easy use.
