Cab Sharing Utility

PROBLEM STATEMENT:
Managing cab sharing among students can be difficult. People often struggle to find cabmates, coordinate departure times, and share driver details. This project aims to solve that problem by providing a simple Python-based utility to store, view and add cab information.



SCOPE OF THE PROJECT:
TO make a useful python powered utility that helps people (especially students) find cabs that go the same place that they want to go to 



TARGET USERS:
college students, who frequently share cabs to campus.  
normal users, who want a simple tool to organize cab sharing without complex apps. 
people in remote areas who find it difficult to find a source to travel. 



HIGH LEVEL FEATURES:
store cabmate details in separate arrays (person, phone, dest(destination) and time).  
add new cab entries with user input.  
an ability to find cabs that go to the same destination.
simple text-based interface for easy use.
